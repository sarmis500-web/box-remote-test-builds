Box Remote privacy policy

Effective September 7, 2026. Applies to the Box Remote phone app and the Box Remote Agent app for TV boxes.

What the apps do

Box Remote turns a phone into a remote control for an Android TV box on the same Wi-Fi network. The phone app sends button presses to the Box Remote Agent app running on the box. The agent turns them into key presses on the box.

Data we collect

None. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no server operated by us. Nothing leaves your Wi-Fi network.

Data stored on your devices

The phone app stores the address of your box and a pairing token so you do not have to pair again. The agent app stores the pairing tokens of phones you have paired. Both stay on the device and are removed when the app is uninstalled.

Accessibility service

The Box Remote Agent uses Android's accessibility service on the TV box to press Back, Home and navigation keys when you press a button on your phone, and to open the app you chose. During setup it may bring up its own setup screen and select its own keyboard so that the remote can work; outside setup it acts only when you press a button. It does not read, record or transmit the content of the screen.

Keyboard (input method)

The Box Remote Agent includes a keyboard so the phone can send arrow keys and typed text to the box. It does not collect what you type. Text typed on the phone goes only to the box on your Wi-Fi.

Permissions

Phone app: network access to reach the box, vibration for button feedback. Agent app: network access to listen for the phone on the local network, and the option to ignore battery optimisation so the remote keeps working.

Children

The apps are not directed at children and collect no personal information from anyone.
