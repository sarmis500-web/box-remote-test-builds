Box Remote privacy policy

Effective September 11, 2026 (updated for usage analytics and the in-app purchase). Applies to the Box Remote phone app and the Box Remote Agent app for TV boxes.

What the apps do

Box Remote turns a phone into a remote control for an Android TV box on the same Wi-Fi network. The phone app sends button presses to the Box Remote Agent app running on the box. The agent turns them into key presses on the box.

Data we collect

There is no account and no sign-in, and no advertising. Button presses and typed text travel only between your phone and your box on your Wi-Fi network.

Usage analytics. Both apps send anonymous usage information to PostHog, an analytics service we use (servers in the United States): which screens and buttons are used and how often, whether setup and pairing succeeded, the box model and Android version, the phone model and operating system version, the app version, and error reports. Each installation is identified by a random ID generated by the app; it is not tied to your name, email, phone number or any account. The service records the approximate location (city level) derived from the network address of the request. This data is used only to understand how the apps are used and to fix problems; it is not sold and not used for advertising. On the phone you can turn this off at any time: Setup tab → "Share anonymous usage data". We never send your Wi-Fi network name, device addresses, pairing tokens, the names you give your boxes, the text you type, or anything shown on the TV screen.

Purchases. The phone app's one-time unlock is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Purchase receipts are verified by RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, under a random app-specific ID so the unlock can be restored on your other phones signed in to the same store account. We do not receive your payment details.

Data stored on your devices

The phone app stores the address of your box and a pairing token so you do not have to pair again. The agent app stores the pairing tokens of phones you have paired. Both stay on the device and are removed when the app is uninstalled.

Accessibility service

The Box Remote Agent uses Android's accessibility service on the TV box to press Back, Home and navigation keys when you press a button on your phone, and to open the app you chose. During setup it may bring up its own setup screen and select its own keyboard so that the remote can work; outside setup it acts only when you press a button. It does not read, record or transmit the content of the screen.

Keyboard (input method)

The Box Remote Agent includes a keyboard so the phone can send arrow keys and typed text to the box. It does not collect what you type. Text typed on the phone goes only to the box on your Wi-Fi.

Permissions

Phone app: network access to reach the box, vibration for button feedback. Agent app: network access to listen for the phone on the local network, and the option to ignore battery optimisation so the remote keeps working.

Children

The apps are not directed at children and knowingly collect no personal information from anyone.
