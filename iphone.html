Box Remote

Your iPhone is the remote

Set up in 3 stepsFor Android TV boxes on your Wi-Fi
1

Install the TV app

  • TV box
  • Its app store
  • Box Remote Buddy for TV
  • Install
  • Open

On the TV box, open its app store and search Box Remote Buddy for TV.

2

Turn on its one switch

  • I understand — continue
  • Open the accessibility list
  • Box Remote Agent
  • On
  • OK

The TV app shows these buttons itself. On the TV the app is called Box Remote Agent. Then follow the TV until it says your phone can control this box.

3

Connect your iPhone

  • Same Wi-Fi
  • Open Box Remote
  • Tap your box
  • Type the 4-digit pairing code

The iPhone finds the box by itself, and the TV shows the 4-digit pairing code. If the iPhone asks about the local network, tap Allow. That's it: your iPhone is the remote.

No app store on your TV box? Ask the person who sold it to you.

Privacy · Works with Android TV boxes running Android 10 and newer.
