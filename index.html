Box Remote

Test build. Works with Android TV boxes on your Wi-Fi. Expires September 21, 2026.

On this Android phone

Become a tester Install Box Remote from Play

Tap the first button, then the second. It opens the Play Store; if it says the item isn't found, wait a few minutes and tap it again. Then open Box Remote, tap your box, and type the 4-digit code the TV shows.

Says you're not eligible? Send the person who gave you this link the Gmail address you use on this phone; they add it to the tester list.

On this iPhone

  1. Look at your own box's setup screen on the TV. It shows an address made of numbers ending in :8484/. Every box has a different one.
  2. Type that exact address in Safari. The remote appears, and its top line names the box it reached. If that is not your box, do not enter a code.
  3. Tap the share button, then Add to Home Screen, so it opens like an app.

The first button you press asks for the 4-digit code the TV shows. The iPhone app is in Apple's TestFlight review; you get an email invite when it clears. This is the same remote in the browser.

On the TV box, once

Do this part from a computer, in a browser signed in to the Google account your box's Play Store uses. On an Android phone these links open the Play Store app, which can only install onto phones.

  1. Open the tester invite for the box app and press Become a tester.
  2. Open the box app's Play Store page, press Install, and under Choose a device pick your box. It may show under a maker's name you don't expect, such as "Google Elite".
  3. Google may first ask you to verify it's you. Do that, then press Install again.
  4. On the TV, the app arrives within about a minute. Nothing after a minute? Open the Play Store on the box once, then look again.
  5. Open Box Remote Agent on the box and follow the steps it shows until it says your phone can control this box.

Says you're not eligible? Send the person who gave you this link the Gmail address your box's Play Store uses; they add it to the tester list.

On a phone

Open this page on the phone you want to use as the remote. Android phones get the Play Store buttons; iPhones get the browser remote.

No Play Store on the box, or in a hurry? The direct download

Had the earlier test build on the box or phone? Uninstall it first. The Play Store version cannot install over it.

  1. On the box, open the green Market tile and install Downloader.
  2. Open Downloader and type this in its address box, then Go, Install, Open:
tinyurl.com/2b6k4yna
  1. Follow the steps the TV shows until it says your phone can control this box.

Android 13 or 14 box and the accessibility switch keeps turning itself off? Settings, Apps, Box Remote Agent, the three-dot menu, "Allow restricted settings", then try again. Play Store installs are never blocked, which is why the Play route above is the main one.

Android phone without Play access: download the phone app directly. The phone will warn that it has not seen this developer before; "Install anyway" is under More details.
